Research Interests

I am broadly interested in the interactions between homotopy theory and geometric topology. More precisely, I am currently interested in the study of the homotopy type of moduli spaces of manifolds, automorphism groups and embedding spaces. During my Ph.D., I have focused on finding analogs of well-established results about even-dimensional manifolds in the context of odd dimensions.


For more on my academic background and contact information, please see my CV.


I was a part of the organising team of the European Talbot Workshop for the 2025 and 2026 editions.
